## What Order Flow is

One shop, one license key, as many screens as you want:

- a **Main** device (Android phone/tablet, Windows laptop, or the web app) keeps the shop's menu, orders, stock and staff **on your own device**;
- **stations** — waiter phones, the kitchen screen, the cashier, the delivery rider, the manager — join Main over the **shop Wi-Fi** in seconds by scanning a QR code. Stations need no extra license;
- if the Wi-Fi is down or devices are far apart, an **encrypted cloud room** connects them over mobile data. The cloud only passes messages along; your shop data stays on your devices.

## Everything a shop needs

**Taking orders**
- Dine-in with a live **table map**, takeaway, delivery, quick retail sale with **barcode scanning**
- Modifiers (size, spice, add-ons), notes, courses (starter / main / dessert), **86 board** for sold-out items
- **Kitchen screen** + kitchen slips; ready / served status flows back to the waiter

**Getting paid**
- Cash (with change), card, wallet, other, complimentary, **split payment**, tips, **customer credit (khata)**
- **Refunds and voids** with manager PIN, receipts by printer, WhatsApp or SMS
- **Deals, combos and happy-hour prices** applied automatically

**Guests order themselves**
- **Table QR menu** — guests scan, browse photos and prices in **English or Urdu**, order, and track their order. No app to install
- **Call waiter** and **Bill please** buttons ring the staff phones
- A table that orders again **joins the same bill**; the kitchen slip prints only the new items
- Works on the shop Wi-Fi, or on mobile data through the cloud room

**Running the shop**
- **Cash drawer handover** — opening float, cash in/out, count note by note, short/over per cashier, printed slip
- **Staff attendance** — clock in/out with a PIN, hours and pay (hourly, daily or monthly), on-shift / break status visible to the owner
- **Stock** that drops as you sell, low-stock signals, buy list, **suppliers and purchase orders**, **wastage log**, **recipe costing and food margin**
- **Reports** — daily and X/Z reports, shifts, best sellers
- **Loyalty points and customers**, **reservations**, **drivers and delivery** dispatch
- **Menu import** — photo or PDF of your existing menu → dishes, prices and categories in one go (read on your device)

**Printing & hardware**
- 58/80 mm thermal printers over **network (port 9100)**, **Bluetooth**, or the **Windows printer list**
- Cash drawer kick, separate kitchen / bar printers, customer display

**Language** — every screen in **English and اردو** (right-to-left), switch any time.

## Why shops choose Order Flow

| | Order Flow | Typical cloud POS |
|---|---|---|
| Internet goes down | Keeps selling, printing and syncing on the shop Wi-Fi (license allows 48 h fully offline) | Often stops or goes read-only |
| Where your data lives | On your own devices | On the vendor's servers |
| Hardware | Your existing Android phones and Windows PCs | Often a dedicated terminal |
| Extra waiter / kitchen screens | Free stations — scan a QR to join | Often a fee per device or user |
| Guest self-ordering | Built in, no app for guests, English + Urdu | Often an add-on |
| Urdu interface | Full Urdu with right-to-left layout | Rare |
| Menu setup | Import from a photo or PDF | Usually typed by hand |
| Support | WhatsApp, a real person, usually within 24 hours | Ticket systems |

## Get started in 5 minutes

1. **Download** — Android: [jathol.org/download](https://jathol.org/download) (or Google Play). Windows 10/11: [jathol.org/download?os=windows](https://jathol.org/download?os=windows) — unzip and run `order_flow.exe`.
2. **Try the demo** on the license screen — a sample café opens so you can tap through everything.
3. **Buy a license** — plans and prices on [jathol.org](https://jathol.org). Enter the key on your Main device.
4. **Add your menu** — type it or import a photo/PDF. Add tables, staff and printers.
5. **Connect stations** — on each extra phone choose *Join as station* and scan Main's QR.

The step-by-step [user guide](https://jathol.org/guide) (English + اردو) has short "watch how it works" clips for the main features.
